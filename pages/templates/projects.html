Optimization & Forecasting • 2026

MealMind – Demand Forecasting & Surplus Redistribution

View Repository

Engineered the machine learning and algorithmic decision backend for an institutional kitchen optimization system, drastically curtailing food waste.

Key Architecture Highlights:

  • Gradient-Boosted Forecasting: Built demand projections with SHAP explainability achieving an impressive 3.5% MAPE on synthetic data, directly feeding a production-sizing optimizer.
  • MILP Surplus Routing: Formulated surplus food redistribution as a Mixed-Integer Linear Program (MILP) using SciPy.
  • Constrained Vehicle Routing: Designed a VRP solver incorporating cold-chain compliance, tight delivery windows, and perishable food expiry limits.
  • Risk Simulation: Integrated a Monte Carlo scenario engine to model demand fluctuations and delivery uncertainties.

Conversational AI & Speech • 2026

MediKiosk – Conversational AI Pipeline for Clinical Intake

View Repository

Designed the speech-to-text and clinical entity extraction pipeline for an automated hospital patient intake system.

Key Architecture Highlights:

  • Speech-to-Text: Integrated low-latency faster-whisper transcription pipeline feeding medical speech directly into LLM extractors.
  • Structured Schema Validation: Leveraged Gemini API extraction paired with strict Pydantic schemas capturing clinical parameters, confidence scores, and symptom negations.
  • Clause-Aware Negation: Developed an algorithmic negation scanner to reliably differentiate positive symptoms from negated reports ("patient denies fever").
  • LLM-Free Clinical Summary: Built a lightweight deterministic summarization engine eliminating unnecessary API overhead.
